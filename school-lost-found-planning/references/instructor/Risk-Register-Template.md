# Risk Register Template

**First entry due:** end of [Week 6](../slides/Week-06-Risk-Quality.md) · **Update #2 due:** end of [Week 11](../slides/Week-11-Change-Management.md) · **Evidence requirement:** the [Week 14 evidence package](Week-14-Evidence-Package-Checklist.md) requires **both** updates, showing the register actually evolved — a register that's identical between Week 6 and Week 11 is treated as a sign it wasn't maintained.

## Response strategies

| Strategy | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Avoid** | Change the plan to eliminate the risk | Drop a risky feature from scope |
| **Mitigate** | Reduce probability or impact | Add extra testing time for a fragile integration |
| **Transfer** | Shift the risk elsewhere | Use a managed service instead of self-hosting |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge and monitor, do nothing active | A low-probability, low-impact risk |

## Week 6 checklist — first real revision

- [ ] Reviewed Sprint 1: which risks *actually* materialized (not what was imagined in Week 2)?
- [ ] Recorded what was done about each — did the response strategy work?
- [ ] Added any new risks only visible after running one real sprint

⚠️ A register with only hypothetical, generic risks ("scope creep," "team conflict") and nothing specific to what actually happened in Sprint 1 does not satisfy the Week 6 milestone.

## Week 11 checklist — update #2

- [ ] Reviewed which Week 6 risks materialized, closed, or are still open
- [ ] Recorded the real scope change processed this week (cross-reference your change record)
- [ ] Added any new risks visible since Week 6
