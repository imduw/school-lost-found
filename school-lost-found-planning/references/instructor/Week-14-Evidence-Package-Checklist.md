# Week 14 — Evidence Package Checklist

**Due:** end of [Week 14](../slides/Week-14-Report-Finalization.md), reviewed in-class the same week (working session, not a new lecture) · **Goal:** catch missing evidence while there's still time to fix it — not at Week 15's defense.

## The checklist

- [ ] **Final report** — synthesizes the semester, doesn't repeat every weekly slide
- [ ] **Repo link** — real commit history, not a single end-of-semester commit
- [ ] **Board export** — Sprint 1 & 2 history, not just a final-state screenshot
- [ ] **Risk register** — both updates ([Week 6](../slides/Week-06-Risk-Quality.md), [Week 11](../slides/Week-11-Change-Management.md)), showing it evolved
- [ ] **Retrospective notes** — Sprint 1 ([Week 7](../slides/Week-07-Resources-Team.md)) and final ([Week 13](../slides/Week-13-Project-Closure.md))
- [ ] **Change record** — the real scope change processed in Week 11
- [ ] **AI tool evidence** — from Week 9's milestone (screenshot/log of real usage)
- [ ] **Acceptance confirmation** — from Week 13's closure

## Report structure (suggested)

1. Project overview (from your Week 2 charter)
2. Methodology chosen and why (Weeks 1-2)
3. Planning summary: WBS, estimation, schedule (Weeks 3-4)
4. Execution summary: sprints run, what shipped (Weeks 5-12)
5. Risk & change management: what actually happened (Weeks 6, 11)
6. AI tool usage: what you tried, what you learned (Weeks 9-10)
7. Closure & lessons learned (Week 13)
8. Reflection: what would you do differently

## Common gaps caught in review

- Evidence that's a **snapshot** instead of a **history** (e.g., one screenshot of a "finished" board, no record of how it got there)
- A risk register that never changed between Week 6 and Week 11 — a sign it wasn't actually maintained
- AI tool "evidence" that's a general description with no actual output attached
- A report written in future tense (reads like a plan) instead of past tense (a record of what happened)
